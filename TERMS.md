These terms apply to everyone who registers or updates a generator in this
repository, by opening a pull request that adds or changes a file under
`generators/`.

## What you grant

The generator stays yours. By registering a version you allow the
kadet-hub maintainers to copy the tree at the pinned commit, publish it as
an OCI artifact under `ghcr.io/kadet-hub/<name>`, show its README, manifest
and metadata on kadet-hub.org, and keep publishing it for as long as it is
listed, all under the license the tree carries. The LICENSE and any NOTICE
file are published unchanged.

## What you confirm

- You have the right to publish the tree under its license, including any
  third-party code and charts it contains.
- The tree contains no secrets, malware or code meant to harm the people
  who use it, and you will report such a problem in a listed version as
  described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- The listing data (name, description, tags, owners) is accurate, and the
  name does not suggest an affiliation you do not have.

## Review and removal

Listing is at the maintainers' discretion. They may refuse a version,
yank it, or remove an entry and its artifacts, in particular after a
justified removal request ([SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md#removal-requests)) or
a confirmed security problem. They tell the owners the reason.

catalog are provided as they are. The checks and the review find known
patterns and obvious problems; they do not guarantee that a generator is
correct, secure or fit for a purpose. You use listed generators at your own
risk and review the compiled output before you apply it.
