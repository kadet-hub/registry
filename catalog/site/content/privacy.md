## What happens when you visit

kadet-hub.org is a static site served by GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, GitHub receives your IP address and the usual request data and, according to GitHub, logs the IP address for security purposes. kadet-hub has no access to these logs. GitHub transfers data to the United States on the basis of its certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the EU standard contractual clauses; see [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The legal basis is the legitimate interest in operating a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The site loads no scripts, styles, fonts or images from other origins, sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking.

## Theme setting

If you use the light or dark toggle, your choice is stored in your browser's `localStorage` under the key `theme`, so the site keeps it on your next visit. Nothing is written before you use the toggle, and the value never leaves your browser. You can delete it at any time in your browser's site data. This storage is strictly necessary for a function you requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

## Data shown in the catalog

The catalog shows the GitHub login of each generator's owners and the star count of its source repository, taken from the public GitHub API when the site is built. Owners submit their GitHub user ID when they register a generator; their login is shown to attribute the generator to them.

## Links to other sites

Links to GitHub, the Kapitan documentation and generator repositories lead to sites with their own privacy notices. Following a link to GitHub, for example to report an issue, is subject to GitHub's terms and privacy statement.

## Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21), and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77), for example the authority of your place of residence. For data that GitHub processes as host, contact GitHub as well.
