description: Open requests for Kapitan generators nobody has published yet, most 👍 first. Pick one to write, or add your 👍 to the one you need.
---

Requests for Kapitan generators that are not listed yet, opened with the
[request form](https://github.com/kadet-hub/registry/issues/new?template=generator-request.yml).
A 👍 on a request moves it up, so add yours to an open request instead of
opening a second one. Anyone can pick a request up and publish the
generator; the [author guide](https://github.com/kadet-hub/registry/blob/main/docs/authors.md)
explains how.

The list is from the last catalog build, at most a day old.
