description: Open requests for Kapitan generators and recipes nobody has published yet, most 👍 first. Pick one to write, or add your 👍 to the one you need.
---

Requests for Kapitan generators that are not listed yet, opened with the
[generator request form](https://github.com/kadet-hub/registry/issues/new?template=generator-request.yml),
and for recipes of applications that kadet-hub/recipes does not have yet,
opened with the
[recipe request form](https://github.com/kadet-hub/recipes/issues/new?template=recipe-request.yml).
A 👍 on a request moves it up, so add yours to an open request instead of
opening a second one. Anyone can pick a request up: the
[author guide](https://github.com/kadet-hub/registry/blob/main/docs/authors.md)
explains how to publish a generator, and
[Adding a recipe](https://github.com/kadet-hub/recipes#adding-a-recipe)
how to write a recipe.

The lists are from the last catalog build, at most a day old.
