How it is checked

The examples CI verifies the signed index and the attestation of every pinned generator, compiles the target, validates the output against the generators' schemas, deploys it to a new kind cluster with the operators above and fails unless {{ .Params.check | markdownify }}. The Chainsaw test holds the assertions.
