    2Verify Customs gate

    kadet-hub

    Before anything is fetched, the consumer workflow downloads kadet-hub's index, verifies its attestation and reads its serial, 147. It looks up each pin: the index must list the digest under the pinned version, and not as yanked. Then gh attestation verify runs once per digest.

    A pass proves that the release workflow of kadet-hub/registry built the artifact with this digest. It does not prove that a generator is harmless in your project, so review the compiled output before you apply it. What a listing covers names what the checks look at.

    kadet-hub
